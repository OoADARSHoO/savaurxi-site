Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve our services. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

Essential Cookies Core website functionality; session management No — required to use the site
Analytics Cookies Understand how visitors navigate our site (Google Analytics) Yes — via cookie banner or browser settings
Marketing Pixels Track ad conversions and retarget audiences (Meta Pixel) Yes — via cookie preferences

You can manage or withdraw cookie consent at any time through your browser settings or our cookie consent tool. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

06

Children's Privacy

Important: Our services are not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from, children under the age of 18.

Since our audience may potentially include minors, we take extra precautions. If you are under 18, you must obtain verifiable parental or guardian consent before providing any personal information to us or using our services.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under 18 without verified parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information. If you believe we may have collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately at secure.communications@savaurxi.tech.

Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection law (DPDP Act, 2023) and other relevant regulations, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your data where it is no longer necessary.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for processing at any time (e.g. unsubscribe from emails).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at secure.communications@savaurxi.tech. We will respond within 30 days.
